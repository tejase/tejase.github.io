Cisco

Lifecycle Management (LCM) Platform

LCM was started from scratch, and I have been on it from the beginning.

  • Led end-to-end engineering and sprint execution as Team Lead and Scrum Master for an enterprise infrastructure lifecycle and security compliance platform.
  • Built and scaled high-performance microservices across Java (Spring Boot) and Rust (Axum), processing real-time ServiceNow data over Apache Kafka to automate OS patch tracking, 90-day SLA enforcement, and executive reporting.
